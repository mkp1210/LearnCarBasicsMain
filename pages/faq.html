Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to help you navigate everyday car ownership metrics.

← Back to Knowledge Centre

We highly recommend pulling out your dipstick to verify fluid stability at least once a month, or before launching into any long-distance highway road trip. This helps you spot silent oil leaks early before internal engine damage can manifest.

For most standard passenger cars, the ideal tire metric floats cleanly between 32 to 35 PSI. Never judge based on the maximum threshold stamped onto the tire walls; instead, locate your specific manufacturer label sticker inside the driver-side door jamb.

For step-by-step visual demonstrations on everyday car maintenance and repairs, we highly recommend checking out ChrisFix's YouTube channel here .

To customize your vehicle's license plate, you must submit an application form to the National Transport Authority (NTA) along with the designated processing fee. Once your requested letter and number combination is approved, you will receive an official authorization certificate to print your new plates. Please don't add slurs or forbidden words to your number plates.
