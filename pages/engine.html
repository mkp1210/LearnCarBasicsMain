How to change Your engine oil

Your engine oil is the lifeblood of your automobile. It provides a slippery, fluid barrier between rapidly moving metallic parts, reducing friction damage and absorbing extreme operating temperatures. Neglecting your oil change intervals leads to oil oxidation, converting clear fluid into a thick, black sludge that chokes oil passages and destroys piston rings.

Step-by-Step: Changing your engine oil

1. Parks your vehicle on flat, level ground and let the engine rest for 10 minutes so all oil pools back down into the pan.
2. Pull out the bright yellow or orange plastic dipstick handle from the front of the block.
3. Wipe the metal probe clean using a lint-free shop towel to expose the indicators.
4. Re-insert the dipstick fully, then draw it out a second time to inspect the oily residue line.
5. Ensure the oil line sits directly between the "MIN" and "MAX" notch points. If it falls below, add small increments of approved weight motor oil immediately.

Difficulty Level: Hard

How to check your Brake fluid level

Your brake fluid is what allows your vehicle to stop, otherwise you and your car can go kaboom.

Step-by-Step: Checking your brake fluid

1. Parks your vehicle on flat, level ground and let the engine rest for 10 minutes so the brake fluid has a chance to cool.
2. Usually found nearest to the windscreen inside the engine bay will be a brake fluid container, their should be a symbol on top or text saying brake fluid.
3. Unscrew the cap and the lid should have a built in level indicator, at this point its important to also ensure the brake fluid is of good quality for which a tester can be used.
4. If its a the correct level no need to change it, if its in need of change drain the brake fluid from the brakes and then refill.
5. Re-insert the dipstick fully, then draw it out a second time to inspect the brake fluid residue line.
6. Ensure the brake fluid line sits directly between the "MIN" and "MAX" notch points. If it falls below, add small increments of approved weight brake fluid immediately.
7. Dont forget to bleed the brakes by pumping them repeatedly to remove air bubbles.
